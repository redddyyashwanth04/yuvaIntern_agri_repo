## Week 1 — Post-Harvest Losses in Agricultural Supply Chains

**Objective:** Research publicly available data on a challenge in the Agriculture &
Agribusiness sector and define a clear, data-driven problem statement.

**Deliverable:** [`Week1/Week1_PostHarvestLosses_ProblemStatement.docx`](./Week1/Week1_PostHarvestLosses_ProblemStatement.docx)

**Summary:** India loses an estimated 18–22% of perishable produce (fruits, vegetables,
livestock produce) between farm and market each year, driven largely by a severe
cold-chain infrastructure gap. The report defines a problem statement around this gap
and proposes data science methodologies (spoilage prediction, price forecasting,
infrastructure-gap clustering) to address it in later weeks.
